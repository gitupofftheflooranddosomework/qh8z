Privacy

Last updated: August 17, 2026.

QH8Z stores account information you provide, the links you create, and operational information needed to run and protect the service. Short-link visits may generate technical analytics such as timestamps, referrers, user-agent-derived device/browser information, and approximate geographic information provided by the redirect engine.

Why we use data

We use this information to provide link management and analytics, secure accounts, investigate abuse, operate subscriptions, diagnose service problems, and comply with applicable law.

Payments

Payment processing is handled by Stripe when paid plans are enabled. QH8Z does not need to store full payment-card details.

Retention and deletion

You can export your account data or delete your account from the QH8Z dashboard. Deleting an account disables its active QH8Z links and removes the account data, subject to limited security, abuse, billing, or legal records we may be required to retain.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@qh8z.com.

This policy is an operational launch draft and should receive jurisdiction-specific legal review before broad public marketing.
