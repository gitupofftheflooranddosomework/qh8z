Automated safety services

Email and payments

We use a configured email delivery provider to send verification, password-reset, and important account messages. Stripe processes payments when paid plans are enabled. We share only the information needed for those providers to perform the requested service.

Cookies

Retention

Active account and link records are retained while your account exists. Session and one-time authentication tokens expire automatically. Operational security, resolved abuse, and billing event records are subject to a configured retention period and may be retained longer when reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, legal obligations, disputes, or enforcement.

Your choices

Security
