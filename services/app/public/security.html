Help keep QH8Z safe.

If you discover a vulnerability in QH8Z itself, please report it privately to security@qh8z.com. Include enough detail to reproduce the issue, affected URLs or components, and your contact information if you want a reply.

Please do

  • Use test accounts and data you control.
  • Stop testing if you access another user’s data or cause service degradation.
  • Give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate before public disclosure.

Please do not

  • Perform denial-of-service testing, destructive testing, social engineering, spam, or automated scanning that meaningfully degrades QH8Z.
  • Access, alter, retain, or disclose data belonging to other users beyond what is necessary to demonstrate a vulnerability.

Bad short links

Phishing, malware, scams, spam, and other harmful destinations should be submitted through the abuse reporting form instead of the security channel.
