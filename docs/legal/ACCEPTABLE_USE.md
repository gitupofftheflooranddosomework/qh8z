# qh8z Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date: August 18, 2026**

This Acceptable Use Policy (**AUP**) applies to every qh8z account, workspace, API request, custom domain, and short link. It forms part of the qh8z Terms of Service.

You may not use qh8z to create, distribute, conceal, facilitate, or materially support content or activity that falls into the categories below.

## Malware and unauthorized access

Prohibited activity includes malware distribution, credential theft, phishing, exploit delivery, command-and-control infrastructure, malicious downloads, ransomware, unauthorized access, traffic interception, or instructions/automation whose primary purpose is compromising systems or accounts without authorization.

## Fraud, scams, impersonation, and deceptive redirects

Do not use qh8z for financial scams, fake support pages, deceptive login pages, fraudulent stores, advance-fee schemes, impersonation intended to deceive, misleading redirects, or other conduct designed to obtain money, credentials, personal information, or access through deception.

## Spam and abusive automation

Do not use qh8z for unsolicited bulk messaging, spam campaigns, evasion of platform anti-spam systems, large-scale abusive automation, artificial traffic generation, or attempts to bypass qh8z rate limits or plan limits.

## Illegal or rights-infringing activity

Do not use qh8z to facilitate activity that is unlawful in a jurisdiction that legitimately applies to the activity or to knowingly distribute content that unlawfully infringes intellectual-property, privacy, publicity, or other rights.

A rights dispute is not automatically treated as abuse merely because one party alleges infringement. qh8z may request sufficient information to identify the link, relevant rights, and basis for the complaint.

## Threats, exploitation, and severe harm

Do not use qh8z to facilitate credible threats of violence, targeted harassment involving severe harm, sexual exploitation of minors, non-consensual intimate imagery, human trafficking, or other content where hosting the redirect would create a serious safety risk or violate applicable law.

## Evasion and abuse of qh8z infrastructure

You may not:

- repeatedly recreate links or accounts that qh8z has suspended for abuse;
- use alternate accounts, custom domains, encoding, redirect chains, or automation to evade enforcement;
- probe or attack qh8z infrastructure without authorization;
- interfere with Service availability or monitoring;
- harvest credentials, secrets, API keys, or non-public information from qh8z or its users;
- falsely present a qh8z link or domain as being endorsed by qh8z; or
- use qh8z primarily as a relay to defeat another service's security or abuse controls.

## Security research

Good-faith security research directed at qh8z should follow the process in `SECURITY.md`. Research that respects stated authorization boundaries and avoids harm is not treated as prohibited merely because it discovers a vulnerability.

## Enforcement

qh8z may block a destination before link creation, suspend or disable a link, restrict an API key, restrict an account/workspace, revoke a custom domain, or terminate access where reasonably necessary to enforce this AUP or protect users and infrastructure.

Enforcement decisions may consider automated reputation checks, qh8z block/allow rules, abuse reports, link behavior, repeat violations, severity, user explanations, and reliable external evidence. Automated safety checks are signals rather than guarantees.

Urgent malware, phishing, fraud, child-safety, or credible-harm cases may be acted on without advance notice. Where practical and safe, users may provide context or request review of an enforcement action through `abuse@qh8z.com`.

## Reporting abuse

Report abusive qh8z links through the qh8z abuse-report workflow or email `abuse@qh8z.com`. Include the qh8z short link, category, relevant context, and evidence that can be safely reviewed. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

False reports submitted intentionally to harass another user or overload the abuse process may themselves violate this AUP.
