# qh8z Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how qh8z collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects personal information when people use qh8z.com, qh8z accounts, short links, APIs, analytics, custom domains, and related services.

## Information we collect

### Account and workspace information

We process information such as email address, account and workspace identifiers, email-verification state, membership roles, API-key metadata, audit-log records, and security/session information. Passwords are stored as salted password hashes; session tokens, verification tokens, and API-key secrets are stored only as cryptographic hashes after issuance.

### Link and analytics information

We process destination URLs, short codes, custom-domain configuration, creation/update timestamps, status information, visit counts, referring URL headers, user-agent headers, and timestamps needed to provide link analytics and protect the Service.

qh8z does not need a visitor account in order to redirect a short link. We do not intentionally require a redirected visitor's name or email address for that redirect.

### Network and security information

We process network and request information needed for security and operations. Rate-limit identifiers derived from IP addresses are salted and hashed before durable storage. Reverse-proxy and application logs may contain request metadata necessary to diagnose outages, enforce abuse controls, and investigate security incidents.

### Payment information

Paid subscriptions are processed by Stripe. qh8z receives payment-related identifiers and subscription state, such as Stripe customer/subscription identifiers, plan status, and billing-period information. qh8z does not store full payment-card numbers.

### Communications and abuse reports

If you contact support, report abuse, submit a privacy request, or report a security issue, we process the information you provide and records needed to respond.

## Why we use information

We use personal information to:

- create, authenticate, and secure accounts;
- operate workspaces, links, redirects, custom domains, APIs, QR codes, and analytics;
- deliver verification and service messages;
- process subscriptions and enforce plan entitlements;
- detect phishing, malware, fraud, abuse, automation, and attacks;
- enforce Terms and the Acceptable Use Policy;
- monitor reliability, troubleshoot failures, back up data, and recover from incidents;
- respond to support, abuse, privacy, and security requests;
- comply with legal obligations and valid legal process; and
- maintain and improve the Service using information appropriate to that purpose.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. qh8z is not designed as an advertising service and does not use redirected visitors' activity to build advertising profiles.

## Service providers

We use service providers where necessary to operate qh8z. At launch these may include hosting/infrastructure providers, Stripe for billing, an SMTP/email provider, Google Web Risk for destination-reputation checks, certificate authorities used through Caddy, object storage for encrypted backups, and monitoring/notification providers.

These providers process information according to their role and their own legal obligations. We seek to send only the information reasonably needed for each service. Destination URLs submitted for shortening may be sent to the configured reputation provider for safety checking.

## International processing

Internet infrastructure and service providers may process information outside the province or country where a user lives. Information processed in another jurisdiction may be subject to lawful access under that jurisdiction's laws. qh8z uses contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service and data involved.

## Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to security, fraud, backup, dispute, accounting, and legal requirements.

At launch:

- active account/workspace/link records are retained while needed to provide the Service;
- Free-plan analytics are exposed for a 7-day lookback and Pro analytics for a 90-day lookback; underlying operational/security records may have a different retention period when needed for security or reliability;
- audit and abuse-enforcement records may be retained longer where necessary to prevent repeat abuse or document an enforcement action;
- encrypted database backups follow the production backup retention schedule and expire automatically under that schedule; and
- payment/accounting records may be retained as required for tax, accounting, chargeback, fraud, or legal obligations.

Deleting a live record does not necessarily remove every encrypted backup copy immediately. Backup copies expire through the normal backup-retention cycle and are not used as an active product database except during recovery.

## Security

qh8z uses safeguards including hashed credentials and tokens, HTTPS, access controls, rate limits, destination-reputation checks, private database networking, file-mounted production secrets, audit logs, monitoring, encrypted offsite backups, readiness checks, and documented incident/restore procedures.

No Internet service can guarantee absolute security. If we discover a qualifying breach, we will investigate, mitigate, document, and provide notifications required by applicable law.

## Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, information about processing, withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent, or deletion where qh8z is not required or permitted to retain the information.

Requests can be sent to `privacy@qh8z.com`. We may need to verify identity before fulfilling a request and may retain a record of the request and response.

You can also stop optional communications using any unsubscribe mechanism provided. Operational, billing, security, and account-verification messages may still be sent when necessary to provide or secure the Service.

## Cookies and local browser storage

qh8z uses an HTTP-only session cookie for browser authentication. The Service may use limited browser storage for interface preferences. qh8z does not require third-party advertising cookies to provide the Service.

## Children

qh8z is a general-purpose business/Internet utility and is not directed to young children. Do not submit personal information about a child where you do not have legal authority to do so.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service, providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate account or Service notice when required.
